Egli \`e scritto in lingua matematica, e i caratteri son triangoli, cerchi, ed altre figure geometriche.

\medskip
\normalfont
It is written in the language of mathematics, and its characters are triangles, circles, and other geometric figures.

\bigskip
\small
Galileo Galilei, \emph{Il Saggiatore} (The Assayer), Rome, 1623,\\
speaking of the great book of nature. English rendering for this book.
