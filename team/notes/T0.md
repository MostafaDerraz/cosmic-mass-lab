## Goal
Show that the engine, under the reference law L0, reproduces Newton plus Coulomb, conserves `rho_mu`, energy and momentum, does not depend on the frame angle, and gives universal free fall. Setups and pass criteria: `docs/05-scenarios-and-tests.md`, section 2, T0. No coupling result counts on an engine commit that fails this gate (D3).

## Pre-registration (commit before any production run)
Committed before the production runs. **Disclosure:** while writing the code I made exploratory runs of every case, so I had seen numbers before writing this. They are listed under Progress, labelled exploratory, and are not the results.

- **Question:** does the engine pass T0.1 to T0.5 as written in `docs/05`?
- **Law(s):** L0 only (`Re(mu) dv/dt = Re[mu (E_cal + v x B_cal)]`), static field (no `B_cal`).
- **Settings (as in stamp.json):** SI units, 2D, `phi_bar` = 79.86 deg (and the six angles of T0.3), velocity Verlet, adaptive step with `eta = 1e-3`, conventions of decision D21.
  - **T0.1a:** Earth and moon presets, separation 3.844e8 m, circular speed of the two-body problem, one period, softening 0.
  - **T0.1b:** proton and electron presets, separation 5.29177e-11 m, circular speed, one period, softening 0.
  - **T0.1c:** two protons at 1e-10 m, at rest, softening 0, run for 3 natural times `sqrt(mu r0^3 / k)` with `k = K e^2 - G_N m_p^2` and `mu = m_p / 2`.
  - **T0.2:** 200 bodies drawn with seed 12345 among electron, proton, neutron; square box of side 2e-7 m with reflecting walls; positions uniform within 98% of the box; Gaussian velocities with spread 1e6 m/s (electrons) and 2e4 m/s (protons, neutrons); 10,000 steps; softening of D10 (1e-3 of the smallest initial separation).
  - **T0.3:** the three cases of T0.1 at 10, 45, 79.86, 90, 135, 170 deg.
  - **T0.4:** Earth preset, fixed, neutral; electron, proton, neutron, dust grain (+1e-16 C), each alone at 7e6 m, at rest; softening 0.
  - **T0.5:** one proton; the field on a 21 by 21 grid of side 2e-9 m centred on it (the proton's own point left out); softening 0.
- **Prediction and why:**
  - T0.1a: period `2 pi sqrt(r^3 / (G_N (M + m)))` = 2,357,430 s = 27.285 days, which is 0.055% below the 27.3 days of the criterion; so the test should pass with about half of its 0.1% margin used. Energy drift far below 1e-6 (a circular orbit at 6283 steps per period gave 4e-13 over 1000 periods in MVP-6).
  - T0.1b: period `2 pi sqrt(mu r^3 / k)` = 1.51942e-16 s, 0.025% below 1.5198e-16 s (the criterion's value is for a fixed proton; the reduced mass shortens it by 0.027%). Pass.
  - T0.1c: separation at the end from the closed form `t = sqrt(mu r0 / 2k) [sqrt(r (r - r0)) + r0 ln((sqrt(r) + sqrt(r - r0)) / sqrt(r0))]`, about 3.54e-10 m. Second-order integrator with steps of at most 1e-3 of the local time scale: expected error of order 1e-7 to 1e-6. **This is the case most likely to fail.**
  - T0.2: mass and charge exactly constant (nothing is absorbed). Energy drift of order 1e-9 (weakly coupled gas, small steps). The bodies' momentum is not conserved if a wall is hit; the momentum of the bodies plus the impulse given to the walls should be conserved to rounding. With steps limited to 1e-3 of the nearest-neighbour distance, 10,000 steps cover only about ten such distances, so few wall hits are expected.
  - T0.3: bit-for-bit identical final states at the six angles, because the Real coordinate of the product does not contain the angle (shown in MVP-1 and MVP-4).
  - T0.4: acceleration `G_N M / r^2` = 8.13447 m/s^2 for the four bodies, equal to rounding (about 2e-16), because force and inertia are both proportional to `m` for a neutral source.
  - T0.5: relative error of `E = -s^2 e` against `K e / r^2` at rounding level (below 1e-14), since the solver evaluates the same formula.
- **Analysis plan (quantities, how pass / fail / finding is judged):**
  - T0.1a, b: period from the unwrapped angle of the relative vector over the run; pass if within 0.1% of the criterion's value and the energy drift (D21: change at the end) is below 1e-6. Also reported: error against the analytic two-body period, largest relative change of the separation.
  - T0.1c: pass if the final separation is within 1e-6 (relative) of the closed form.
  - T0.2: pass if the mass and charge drifts are exactly 0, and the energy drift and the drift of (momentum of bodies + impulse on walls) are below 1e-6. The bodies-only momentum drift and the number of wall hits are reported.
  - T0.3: pass if, for each angle, the final positions and velocities, the step counts and the final energies of the three cases are identical, byte for byte, to those at the first angle.
  - T0.4: pass if each relative error and the largest relative difference between bodies are below 1e-12.
  - T0.5: pass if the largest relative error on the grid is below 1e-6, for `E` and for `g`.
  - Convergence (T0.1, T0.2): the same quantities at `eta/2` and `eta/4`; for T0.2 also with 100 and 400 bodies. Reported, with the change between `eta/2` and `eta/4`.
  - A failure is reported as a failure. No setting is changed after seeing the results without saying so here.
- **Changes to the plan (with date and reason):** 2026-11-10: the first production runs (engine commit `08b5b1e`, all five passing) wrote 15 MB of time series, too much for the repository. I changed only the writing (at most about 500 rows per case are kept on disk; the analysis still uses every row), discarded those outputs without committing them, and re-ran everything on the new commit. No setting, criterion or analysis changed; the measured numbers are the same.
