## Pre-registration (commit before any production run)
Committed before any code of L1a or of these tests was written or run. No exploratory run was made.

- **Question:** under the hypothesis L1a (the Composer coordinate read as a second force along the field, strength lambda = 1), does the engine give the book's two numbers?
- **Law(s):** L1a, a **hypothesis** (D1): force = `Re(mu E_cal) + lambda s Com(mu E_cal)`, with `Com(mu E_cal) = q g + m e + 2 s cos(phi_bar) q e` (the product of the compendium; `m e = -m E / s^2`). T9.1: all terms, lambda = 1, sign +. T9.3: the term `s q g` only (L5a), lambda = 1, sign +.
- **Settings (as in stamp.json):** SI, 2D, `phi_bar` = 79.86 deg, softening 0 (D21), no time integration: the forces are evaluated once at the starting positions.
  - **T9.1:** a proton fixed at the origin; a neutron at 1e-10 m, at rest.
  - **T9.3:** an electron alone, at rest, in a uniform background `g` of 9.80665 m/s^2 (the conventional standard acceleration of gravity; the book writes `g` without a value).
- **Prediction and why (analytic):**
  - T9.1: the neutron has `q = 0`, so only the term `m e` acts: Composer force `s m_n e = -(m_n / s) E`, with `E = -s^2 e` the proton's Coulomb field. The neutron responds as a charge `q_eff = -m_n / s` = -(1.67492750e-27 / 1.16043e10) C = -1.4434e-37 C = **-9.009e-19 e**, which the book rounds to 9.0e-19 e. `q_eff` is negative and the proton positive, so the neutron is attracted, with a force about 9.0e-19 of the Coulomb force between two elementary charges.
  - Against the measured neutron charge (-0.2 +/- 0.8)e-21 e (Baumann et al. 1988, as cited by the book): 9.009e-19 / 0.8e-21 = about 1126 standard errors ("about eleven hundred"), and the largest share allowed at two standard errors is (0.2 + 2 x 0.8)e-21 / 9.009e-19 = **0.0020** ("below about two parts in a thousand").
  - T9.3: Composer force `s q g`, the force of an electric field `E_eq = s g` = 1.16043e10 x 9.80665 = **1.1380e11 V/m**, which the book rounds to 1.14e11 V/m.
- **Analysis plan (quantities, how pass / fail / finding is judged):**
  - T9.1: `q_eff` = (Composer force on the neutron) . E / |E|^2, with E the Coulomb field of the proton at the neutron, both taken from the engine (field solver and law). Pass if `|q_eff| / e` is within 0.05e-19 of 9.0e-19 (it rounds to the book's value) and `q_eff` equals `-m_n / s` to 1e-12 relative. Reported: the number of standard errors, and the largest lambda allowed at two standard errors, to compare with the book's "about 0.002"; a check that the response is proportional to lambda (lambda = 0.002).
  - T9.3: `E_eq` = (Composer force on the electron) / q. Pass if `|E_eq|` is within 0.005e11 of 1.14e11 V/m (it rounds to the book's value) and equals `s g` to 1e-12 relative. Reported: the direction (along `g` for a positive charge), and the Real force `m g` beside it.
  - Both are `measurement-check` results: they reproduce numbers derived in the book and compare them with cited measurements. They say that L1a with lambda = 1 is excluded by the neutron, as the book states; they are not findings about nature beyond that.

What these numbers say, each labelled:
- **Mathematics (the engine does what L1a says):** the Composer force on a neutral body of mass `m` in an electric field `E` is `-(m / s) E`; on a charge `q` in gravity `g` it is `s q g`. The engine gives both to rounding. With lambda = 0.002 the neutron's response is 0.002 times as large, to 1e-12.
- **Measurement (cited by the book, not checked by me against the papers):** the charge of the free neutron is (-0.2 +/- 0.8)e-21 e (Baumann et al., Phys. Rev. D 37, 1988; also Bressi et al. 2011 and the Particle Data Group 2024 in the book's bibliography).
- **Comparison:** under L1a with lambda = 1 the neutron would respond as -9.009e-19 e, which is 1126 standard errors from the measured value (the book: "about eleven hundred"). The largest lambda compatible with the measurement at two standard errors is **0.0020** (the book: "below about two parts in a thousand"). So L1a at its natural strength is excluded by measurement, as the book states; this is the reason for the gate T9.
- T9.3 has no direct measurement to compare with here: the book says the effect would be hidden by the metal in the experiment it cites. The number 1.14e11 V/m is reproduced; the Composer force on the electron would be 2.0e21 times its weight.

Surprises: none. Limits:
- Both tests evaluate forces at one instant. Nothing is integrated under L1a here, and no claim is made about how a universe with L1a evolves.
- T9.1 does not depend on the frame angle (the neutron has no charge, so the charge share is absent); checked at 120 deg.
- T9.3 uses the conventional 9.80665 m/s^2; with 9.81 the field is 1.1384e11 V/m. Both round to the book's value.
- The energy reported by the runner is the L0 energy; under L1a it is not a conserved quantity of the law (see `team/notes/MVP-7.md`).
