## Results
Mathematics only (compendium, section "General Numbers"), checked by the tests:
- Rule of the Square `G^2 = 2 s cos(phi_bar) G - s^2`: exact in mpmath at 50 digits, for 6 angles and 3 lengths.
- `z * (1/z) = 1`: to 1e-48 in exact mode, and to 1e-12 relative in floats on 3,600 random values in 18 frames.
- Independent check through the bridge to C (`z -> Sh z + i Ht z`): products, quotients and sums in the leaning frame equal the complex ones to 1e-11 relative or better.
- `Re[(a+bG)(c+dG)] = ac - s^2 bd` is the same floating-point number at every frame angle from 10 to 170 degrees.
- Measurement, restated: `s = sqrt(K/G_N) = 1.16043e10 kg/C` to 6 digits and `m_S = e s = 1.85921e-9 kg`, from the CODATA 2022 values.

Results of MVP-2 (mathematics, unless marked):
- `GNArray` and `GeneralNumber` agree on 10,000 random values in 8 frames for `+ - * /`, inverse and conjugate: largest relative gap at most 1e-15 (the operations are written in the same order, so the bits are the same).
- The identities of the readings hold on arrays in 10 frames (section "General Numbers", paragraphs "Readings", "Conjugate, skew flip, division", "Laws of the parts"). `abs(zw) = abs(z) abs(w)` **is an identity** in the compendium ("The norm is multiplicative"); it is tested to 1e-12 relative in floats and to 1e-47 at 50 digits.
- Section "Cosmic Mass": the full product of two cosmic masses, for the 100 pairs of presets; `G_N Re(mu_a mu_b) = G_N m_a m_b - K q_a q_b` to 1e-14 relative; `Re(mu_a mu_b)` is the same floating-point number at the 33 frame angles of the sweep, while `Com` changes with the angle; the full product `mu E_cal` in both coordinates, on 500 random bodies and 3-vectors in 4 frames.
- The matter presets equal the table of `docs/05`, section 0 (measurement, as quoted there).

Choices made in MVP-2:
- **`Com(z)` returns the coordinate `b`**, as `ARCHITECTURE.md`, section 5, says. The compendium writes the Composer part as `b G`; the "Laws of the parts" hold with the coordinate.
- **`CosmicMass.mu(frame)` takes the frame** (the class diagram had `mu()`): a body is a mass and a charge, and becomes a General Number only in a frame.
- **Vector fields use NumPy broadcasting**: N numbers times an `(N, d)` field is `mu[:, None] * field`.
- **The dust grain is two presets**, `dust grain +` and `dust grain -`, for the two signs of the table. Charged planets ("or measured ...") are made with `with_charge(q)`.
- **`GNArray` is float only**; exact mode stays with `GeneralNumber`.

Choices made in MVP-1:
- **The product is written so that `z * w == w * z` bit for bit** (`b d` is computed once). The real part does not use the angle at all.
- **Exact mode** is not a flag: a frame and coordinates given as `mpmath.mpf` run every operation at the working precision of mpmath.
- **`GeneralNumber.norm_sq()` and `conjugate()`** are in `gn.py` because the inverse needs them (compendium, "Conjugate, skew flip, division"). The readings `Re`, `Com`, `Sh`, `Ht`, `abs` remain task E1.4 (`readings.py`).
- **Frame angles are refused outside the open interval 0 to 180 degrees**, where `sin(phi_bar) = 0` and the plane collapses to a line.
- **Contracts (ARCHITECTURE.md, section 10):** the `Frame` fields are unchanged. Methods of `Frame` and the signatures of `GeneralNumber` were added; nothing existing changed.
